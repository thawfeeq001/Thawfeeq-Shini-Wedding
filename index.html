Our Story

Every love story begins somewhere ordinary. Ours began with a conversation that was meant to be short and somehow never ended. There was no thunder and no grand gesture, only the quiet realisation that the day felt lighter when the other one was in it — and that neither of us wanted the conversation to stop.

After that came the small things nobody photographs well — long drives with the windows down, playlists argued over at every junction, tea that went cold because we were still talking. Those unhurried hours taught us something simple. Home was not a place we were travelling towards. It had quietly become a person.

We learned each other slowly, the way you learn a favourite song. Which corner table she prefers. How he goes quiet before he laughs hardest. The particular silence that means everything is fine, and the one that means it isn't. Love, it turned out, is mostly attention — paid patiently, and returned.

Somewhere between the ordinary evenings and the ones we planned for weeks, the question of forever stopped feeling like a question. It was simply the shape the days were already taking. We told our families. They smiled as though they had been waiting for us to catch up with what they had known all along.

There is a particular kind of happiness in being fully known and chosen anyway. It is not loud. It arrives in the middle of a normal Tuesday and stays. We have been carrying it quietly for a while now, and this year we decided it deserved a celebration — with every person who helped it grow.

We began collecting places. A stretch of water under a pink sky. A hill road that took twice as long as the map promised. None of them were remarkable on their own; they became ours because we were there together, and because we kept coming back to talk about them long after the light had gone.

People ask how we knew. There was never a single moment to point to, only a long accumulation of small ones — the message sent for no reason, the door held open, the plan quietly rearranged. Certainty did not arrive. It was built, patiently, by two people who kept showing up.

So this is where the story pauses and the invitation begins. The water was calm that evening, the light was gold, and neither of us said very much. We did not need to. Some things you simply agree on without speaking, and then spend the rest of your life keeping.

And so, from one quiet conversation to a day surrounded by everyone we love — thank you for being part of how we got here.

A day where two families became one.
